## 描述（Description）

```
Duck Duck Extraction 3D is a single-player, top-down extraction shooter in a colorful world of armed ducks.

ENTER THE ZONE
Drop into six timed maps, each with its own layout, enemies, and boss. Search crates, computer towers, and safes for weapons, armor, and supplies. Watch the clock — when it runs out, everything you carried is lost.

EXTRACT OR LOSE IT ALL
Every raid is a risk-and-reward decision. Bring a loadout, plan your route, decide whether to challenge the boss, and leave while your backpack is full. Extract successfully and your gear returns to your stash. Fall in battle or run out of time and you lose the gear you brought in and everything you found.

SIX ZONES, SIX BOSSES
- Duck Island — lakeside farms and a military base guarded by the Goose Boss.
- Saltmist Harbor — waterways and cargo yards patrolled by Captain Ironhook.
- Redforge Works — foundry lines and furnace halls held by the Furnace Foreman.
- Rotleaf Hatchery — swamps and nests where the Broodqueen summons endless chicks.
- Frostedge Outpost — frozen hangars and a radar station. Face the Frostfeather Marshal and Ironwall Commander together in a double-boss fight.
- Duck King Mausoleum — royal corridors and twin vaults, home of the Duck King and his four rotating attacks.

THREE CHARACTERS
Sunny (balanced), Ironhead (heavy armor), and Swiftwing (fast scout). Each changes your speed and armor rules. Switch freely between raids.

GEAR, UPGRADES, AND PROGRESSION
Manage a stash, buy weapons and armor, and apply stackable mods — recoil control, armor-piercing rounds, extended magazines, and more. Six armor tiers, character upgrades, consumables, and throwables add depth to every loadout. A local leaderboard tracks your best runs.

VEHICLE PROVING GROUND
Take the fight to a dedicated demo area: drive main battle tanks and armored vehicles, and pilot fighter jets. Touch controls included.

MADE FOR MOBILE
Dual virtual sticks, auto-fire, and on-screen action buttons. Portrait and landscape adapt automatically. All music and sound effects are generated in real time — no downloads, no ads, no account required.

No network requests. Your progress stays on your device.
```

## 审核备注（App Review Information）

```
This is a fully offline, single-player game. No account, login, or network connection is required. No ads and no in-app purchases.

Review path: launch the app -> tap "Start" -> play the free training raid (move with the left stick, aim and auto-fire with the right stick) -> search the supply crate -> defeat the chicks -> stand in the green extraction zone for 6 seconds.

The full game and all six zones are available from the start. No demo account or credentials are needed.
```
