Summary

Duck Duck Extraction 3D is a fully offline, single-player game. It does not connect to the internet, does not require an account or login, contains no advertising, no analytics, no in-app purchases, and no third-party SDKs. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Your game progress is stored only on your device.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following information locally on your device (browser-style local storage inside the app) so your progress persists between sessions:

  • Game progress: stash balance, successful extractions, raids played, best score, and completed training.
  • Your inventory and loadout: owned weapons, armor, medical supplies, throwables, and equipped items.
  • Settings: selected character, sound on/off, and language preference.
  • Local leaderboard entries: score, outcome, loot value, kills, time, date, and the map/character used.

This information never leaves your device. It is not uploaded to us or to any third party. You can remove it at any time by deleting the app.

Data we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or postal address.
  • No device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or persistent user IDs.
  • No precise or approximate location.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or health data.
  • No payment information — there are no purchases in this app.
  • No browsing history or usage analytics.

Network access

The app makes no network requests. All music and sound effects are generated in real time on your device, and all graphics are drawn locally. No external content is downloaded.

Third parties

The app does not integrate any third-party SDK, advertising network, analytics provider, or social login. No third party receives any data from this app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes

If this policy is updated, the effective date above will be changed. Because the app collects no data, no further notice is required for the changes described here.
