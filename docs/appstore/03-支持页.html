Getting started

On first launch, start the free training raid. Move with the left stick, aim and auto-fire with the right stick, approach a supply crate and tap Search, defeat the blocking chicks, then stand in the green extraction zone for six seconds. After training, you can choose any zone and bring your own loadout.

Controls

  • Move: drag the left half of the screen.
  • Aim & fire: drag the right half; the outer ring auto-fires.
  • Search: tap the Search button near a container.
  • Actions: on-screen buttons for roll, reload, heal, throw, switch throwable, switch weapon, and backpack.
  • Language: tap the EN / 简中 button in the top-right corner of the menu.

Frequently asked questions

Is an internet connection required?
No. The game is fully offline. Music and sound effects are generated on your device.

Do I need an account?
No account or login is required. Progress is saved locally on your device.

How do I reset my progress?
Delete the app and reinstall it. This clears the local save and leaderboard. This cannot be undone.

Will switching devices keep my progress?
No. Saves are stored only on the device where you play and do not sync between devices.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. The game has no ads, no in-app purchases, and no subscriptions.

The app will not start / crashes.
Try closing the app fully and reopening it, then restart your device. If the problem continues, email [[SUPPORT EMAIL]] with your device model, iOS version, and a description of what happened.
